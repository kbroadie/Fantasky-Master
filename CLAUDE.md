# Fantasky Master

Fantasy league for the TV show *Taskmaster*. Players pick one contestant per episode and score Show points (the contestant's score) and League points (5-4-3-2-1 by finishing place). Rules: `README.md`. Every calculation: `FANTASKY_MASTER_EXPLAINED.md`.

- **Live app:** https://kbroadie.github.io/Fantasky-Master/app/ (GitHub Pages serves `main` as-is).
- **Legacy site:** `index.html` at the root. It is frozen; don't read it or build on it.

## Layout

```
data/fantasky_master_data.csv   ← the ONLY league data (hand-edited; schema in data/README.md)
data/taskmaster_stats.csv       all-time stats for every contestant, S1–22 (imported, never hand-edited)
app/index.html                  sticky top bar (masthead + 3 tabs), the three pages and the footer
app/styles.css                  all styles; mobile-first, wider layouts in @media at the end
app/js/main.js                  renders all pages at load, tabs, swipers, the Standings rows, series toggle, countdown, routing (#/series/page/arg)
app/js/ui.js                    shared helpers ($, esc, ord, framed…) and `state`
app/js/views/{table,episodes,cast}.js   one file per tab (Standings, Episodes, Cast); each returns HTML strings
app/js/podium-fx.js             canvas effects on episode podiums (winner's gold light, last place's stink gas), and the gold light on the first-place Cast header
app/js/heroes.js                presentation only: each series' cast photos (a group photo, or a hero photo per contestant), and each contestant's eyes and head size in them (the Cast profile's face)
app/js/league.js                pure scoring engine (derive) — must match the systems doc
app/js/csv.js                   CSV → series objects
app/js/alltime.js               loads taskmaster_stats.csv: the radar baseline, all-time record badges, profile facts
app/js/edit.js                  edit mode: picks and scores entered on the page, saved to the CSV through the GitHub API
app/js/wiki.js                  reads an episode's scores from the Taskmaster Wiki's API (for edit mode)
app/js/checks.js                the CSV's rules, shared by check-data and edit mode (which checks before saving)
tools/check-data.mjs            validates the CSV (checks.js) + worked-example regression + cross-check against the stats (no deps)
tools/import-stats.mjs          regenerates taskmaster_stats.csv from the all-time stats Google Sheet
tools/screenshots.mjs           Playwright shots of every view at 390px and 1440px → shots/
```

## UX framework

The user's v1 prototype is the model: fast, clean, obvious navigation. The aim is to make it fun and emotionally engaging without making anyone think harder.

- **Top bar** (sticky, frosted glass):
  - The brand.
  - The series number, a gold chip with a swap icon: tap it to switch series.
  - One line of text counting down to the next episode: "Ep 5 airs in 5d 18h" (no pill or background; the numbers are gold). The air date and time are on the episode page, not here. Compact, it's just the gold time (on request): "Ep 5 airs in" (`#cd-what`) steps out of the flow and fades where it stood; "Series 22 complete" stays whole.
  - Below them, three segmented tabs, with a gold panel that slides behind the active one. Their labels are 12px DM Mono (up from 11, on request: they're the most-used buttons).
  - On scroll it compacts to one thin line plus label-only tabs (`--hdr-h`, `--tabs-h`… in `:root`; `--bar-h` is its open height, `--bar-d` what compacting saves). The bar is `position: fixed` over a spacer of its open height (`main`'s padding-top), so compacting never re-lays out or moves the page (on request: animating the page's layout made it choppy; and if the page moved, scroll anchoring would flip the bar open and shut). Only the bar's own few boxes are laid out as it compacts; its frosted glass is its own layer (`.topbar::before`) at the open height that slides up by `--bar-d` with a transform, so the blur is never re-rendered at a new size. Keep it transform-based.
  - **Hides while scrolling down** (on request, like Safari's address bar): past the first 120px, 12px of scrolling down slides the compact bar up out of sight (`.topbar.hidden`, a transform; `setHidden` in `main.js`) and any 8px back up brings it back; the overscroll bounce at either end is ignored, and it shows again if the keyboard focuses into it. The sticky strip stays, sliding up to the top (its 32px band just under the safe area). What's typed is kept in a draft and applied when focus leaves the table (`commit`), so moving between boxes never redraws the page under the keyboard; empty tasks are dropped, and a task with no name or a score that isn't 0–10 or DQ is outlined and not applied. Save applies any draft first, and `refresh` puts focus back on the same box after a redraw (`data-fk`). Under the title a "Get scores from the wiki" button (before broadcast, "Get title from the wiki") fills in the table (`fetchEpisode` in `wiki.js`: the Series page lists each episode's page; its `tmtable` gives each task's type (Prize/Team/Live labels, "Team Live" counts as Live), scores, split-task parts (max of the parent and the parts' sum), bonus rows (added) and tiebreak ✔). The data file's task names and DQs are kept (the wiki often shows a DQ as 0); a placeholder title is filled in too. Before broadcast it fetches only the title, from the Series page's episode list (`fetchTitle` in `wiki.js`; the wiki lists titles before broadcast).
  - Every change is an op replayed onto the file: at once onto the page's copy (the page re-renders via `refresh` in `main.js`), and on Save onto a fresh copy from GitHub, so nobody else's edits are lost (a conflict retries). The result must pass `checkData` (the rules CI runs) before it's sent; warnings (a missing tiebreak) ask first. Rows go where a person would put them: scores right after their episode row, picks at the end of the episode's block in player order. `parseRows`/`toCSV` in `csv.js` round-trip the file byte for byte, so untouched rows never change.
  - The CSV loads with `cache: "no-cache"` (no preload), so a save shows for everyone once Pages redeploys (about a minute).
- **Times are local:** every date, time and countdown uses the device's time zone and locale (`fmtDay`, `fmtWhen` in `ui.js`). Never hard-code London. Episodes air at 10pm London time (`AIR_TZ`, `AIR_HOUR`); `airInstant` turns that into an exact instant with daylight saving applied (checked: BST episodes are 21:00 UTC, GMT ones 22:00, so the week between the UK and US clock changes lands an hour later in the US).
- **Icons:** task types (Prize, Filmed, Team, Live) use one monoline SVG set (`ICON_PATHS` / `icon()` in `ui.js`): 16px grid, 1.5 stroke, gold. Don't mix in emoji; they render in clashing styles. Emoji are only for the 👑/🏆/crown badges.
- **Nothing smaller than 11px.** Use weight and colour for hierarchy.
- **Standings rows** must not be compositing layers of their own: giving each one `will-change: transform` doubled the layers, and iOS then dropped rows' text after scrolling up and down.
- **Emotion comes from faces, gold and colour, not motion.** Use framed portraits, a crown for the winner, gold for first place, gold for a pick that won, and contestant accent colours. The only decorative motion is the episode podium effects and the drifting light on the scoring cards (all requested; see above), which stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Otherwise transitions are only for navigation and touch feedback, such as the tab slide, the row expanding, the week slides and an opened journey drawing in.

## Conventions

- **No personalisation:** there is no "you", player picker or planner (removed on request). There are three tabs only.
- **No build step, no framework, no runtime dependencies.** Native ES modules; `package.json` is for tooling only.
- **Views** are functions returning template strings. Escape user and CSV text with `esc()`, or `rich()` to allow `<strong>`. Interactions go through delegated listeners in `main.js`.
- **Contestants** always appear as full gold-framed portraits (`framed()`), never cropped circles.
- **Fonts:** the v1 set, loaded from Google Fonts, one job each:
  - `--ff-head` Bungee: headings and the brand. It has one weight; keep it at 400.
  - `--ff-name` Fredoka: player and contestant names.
  - `--ff` Inter: body text and every score.
  - `--fm` DM Mono: labels. Only 400 and 500 are loaded.
  - `font-synthesis: none` is set, so never ask for a weight that isn't loaded.
  - Use tabular figures for numbers.
- **Terms:** use **Show** and **League** points, as in README.md.
- **Imgur images:** the page sets `referrer: no-referrer` because Imgur blocks some referrers. Portraits load as `…m.webp`.
